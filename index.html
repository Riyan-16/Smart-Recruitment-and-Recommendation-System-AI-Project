How the ranking works

1

You list the required skills

Each skill is cleaned up and compared in lowercase, so "python" and "Python" count as the same.

2

We check every candidate

Matching skills are counted against the full list of requirements to get a coverage percentage.

3

You get a ranked shortlist

The highest coverage comes first. A matching preferred role breaks ties between equal scores.
